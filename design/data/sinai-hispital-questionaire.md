# Sinai Hospital Questionnaire

**Purpose:** Help us understand how Sinai Hospital manages stock, sales, billing, and tax so that salmon can be built to fit your actual workflow — not a generic retail model.

**Who should fill this in:** Ideally the person who oversees pharmacy/dispensary stock, plus whoever handles billing and KRA filings. It's fine to split it across people.

**Time needed:** ~20–30 minutes. Skip anything that doesn't apply and note why.
Any extra information to be Written in the extra leafs provided and should reference the question (ie. A1, B6, ...).

---

## A. About the business

1. How many branches or locations does Sinai Hospital operate? (main hospital, clinics, pharmacies, labs?)
2. Roughly how many staff would use a POS or billing system daily?
3. Which roles need separate access? (cashier, pharmacist, lab tech, accountant, admin, doctor?)
4. Do you currently use any software for stock, billing, or tax? If yes, which, and what's the biggest problem with it?

## B. Pharmacy & dispensary stock

5. Do you track medicines by **batch number** and **expiry date**, or just by product?
6. How do you currently know when a drug is about to expire? What happens when it does?
7. Do you dispense against a **patient prescription record**, or just sell the item?
8. Are any drugs **controlled substances** that need a special audit trail?
9. Do you move stock between departments (e.g. main store → ward → theatre → pharmacy)? How is that recorded today?
10. How often do you do a **stock take**, and how long does it take?

## C. Billing & payments

11. Do you bill **insurance / NHIF / SHA / private insurers**? If yes, how do you reconcile what they owe vs. what's paid?
12. Do you offer **credit to corporate clients** (e.g. an employer paying for staff treatment)? How is that tracked?
13. For inpatients, do you issue **interim bills** during the stay and a **final invoice** at discharge?
14. Do you take **deposits or partial payments** per patient? How are balances tracked?
15. Which payment methods do you accept? (M-Pesa C2B/STK/Till/Paybill, cash, card, bank transfer, insurance?)

## D. Services vs. goods

16. Besides physical items, do you charge for **services** (consultations, procedures, lab tests, imaging, bed fees)? 
17. Should these services appear on the same invoice as pharmacy items, or separately?
18. Do services need stock tracking, or are they purely billable line items?

## E. Tax & compliance

19. Which of your items/services are **VAT-exempt**? How do you currently classify them?
20. Do you issue **eTIMS invoices** for every sale, including insurance-billed ones? Who is the buyer on those invoices?
21. What **daily, weekly, or monthly reports** do you currently produce for KRA or internal audit?
22. Do you produce a **Z-report / closing report** per cashier or per till? Per department?

## F. Hardware & environment

23. What hardware do you already have? (receipt printers, label printers, barcode scanners, cash drawers, customer displays)
24. Do you need **label printing** for drug batches or patient items?
25. Do you scan **barcodes at the point of dispensing** to prevent wrong-drug errors?
26. How reliable is your power and internet? Do you have a UPS or generator?
27. If the network drops, how long can you keep operating before it becomes a problem?

## G. Reporting & pain points

28. What report do you wish you had but don't?
29. What's the single most frustrating part of your current stock or billing process?
30. If you could change one thing about how you manage stock and billing, what would it be?

## H. Anything else

31. Is there anything we didn't ask that you think we should know?

## [NOORBHAI & SONS HARDWARE](https://www.facebook.com/noorbhaihardware/)

> The first business that welcomed us is [noorbhai & sons hardware](https://www.facebook.com/noorbhaihardware/).

> Noorbhai was welcoming to us, even though the area was very busy, we had and opportunity to have a discussion with them.

> The business used another Database system and later migrated to another ( which they were using up to the time we had contact with them ).

### problems with the previous database system

> the reasons why they migrated to the new database system are as follows

#### bad pricing

> Noorbhai had several branches and due to logistics (ie. transport, ...) the products in each branch varied. The previous database system could not properly handle prices and the same price was tagged across all branches.

##### Why change?
- Bad pricing led to sharp losses and further bugetting frustrations.
- customer churn.
    > customers chose cheaper options from other hardwares if all the products were given the highest price accross branches

#### bad inventory management

> if one product missed from one branches, the previous database system flagged it as missing across all branches, even when they were not actually missing.

##### why change?

- This led to losses of restocking existing products and created perpetual `dead stock`
- The business could not easily know which products to restock.

#### kra and tax filings

> The previous system did not automatically file taxes.

##### why change?

- The new system offered automatic tas reconciliations and filings, this offerd a very strong paper trail for the business and the customers to know how much to submit to [The Kenya Revenue Authority](https://www.kra.go.ke/) without the strin of maunal reconciliations.

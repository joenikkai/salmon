# Noorbhai & Sons Hardware Questionnaire

**Who should fill this in:** Ideally the owner/manager, plus whoever handles stock, billing, purchasing, and KRA filings. It is fine to split it across people.

**Time needed:** ~45–60 minutes. Skip anything that doesn’t apply and note why.  
Any extra information should be written on extra sheets and should reference the question, e.g. **D4**, **F12**, etc.

---

## A. About the business & branches

1. What is the official business name and trading name?
2. How many branches does Noorbhai & Sons operate? Where are they?
3. Is there a main warehouse, central store, or head office? How does it relate to branches?
4. Who owns or manages each branch? Who makes final decisions on pricing, purchasing, credit, and discounts?
5. Roughly how many staff work per branch, and what roles do they have?
6. What are your normal operating hours and peak busy periods?
7. What software or database do you currently use for stock, sales, and tax?
8. What system did you migrate from, and why did you leave it?
9. What works well in the current system? What still frustrates you?
10. What should Salmon do that your current system cannot do?

## B. Users, roles & security

11. Which roles need separate access? (owner, branch manager, cashier, storekeeper, accountant, auditor, sales rep, delivery driver)
12. What should each role be allowed to see and do?
13. Who can change prices? Who can approve discounts?
14. Who can approve credit sales, stock adjustments, write-offs, and inter-branch transfers?
15. Do you need an audit trail for price changes, stock changes, invoices, refunds, and user logins?
16. Should a user see only their own branch, or all branches?
17. Do you need consolidated reports across all branches?
18. Do you need multiple users on one computer, or one user per device?

## C. Products & catalog

19. What product categories do you sell? (cement, steel, plumbing, electrical, paint, tools, fasteners, etc.)
20. How do you identify each product today? SKU, barcode, supplier code, description, or something else?
21. Do you need product photos, descriptions, or specifications?
22. What units of measure do you use? (piece, bag, kg, tonne, metre, roll, packet, bundle)
23. Can one product have multiple units? For example, cement by bag vs. pallet, or cable by metre vs. roll.
24. What variants matter? Brand, size, grade, colour, thickness, length, weight?
25. Do you use barcodes? Do you need to print labels?
26. Do any products need batch numbers, expiry dates, or serial numbers? (paint, chemicals, adhesives, tools with warranty)
27. Do you sell kits or bundles? Do you allow substitutes when an item is out of stock?
28. Do you sell non-stock or special-order items?

## D. Pricing

29. How do you currently set prices? Cost-plus, supplier price, competitor price, manual decision?
30. Do prices differ by branch? Why? How often do they change?
31. Do prices differ by customer type? (retail, contractor, wholesale, credit customer)
32. Do you offer quantity discounts, promotions, or seasonal pricing?
33. Are cashiers allowed to negotiate or override prices? Who approves it?
34. How should price changes be handled across branches? Central price list, branch-specific, or both?
35. Do prices have effective dates? Should old invoices keep old prices?
36. Which items are VAT standard-rated, exempt, or zero-rated?
37. Do you charge separately for delivery, transport, labour, or loading?
38. What rounding rules do you use? Do you handle M-Pesa charges separately?

## E. Inventory & stock control

39. Where is stock held? Branch stores, central warehouse, transit, or all of these?
40. Is stock tracked per branch only, or also by shelf/bin/location?
41. How do you receive stock today? Supplier invoice, delivery note, GRN, manual entry?
42. How do you handle short deliveries, damaged goods, and returns to supplier?
43. How do you handle customer returns, refunds, and exchanges?
44. Do you allow negative stock? Do you accept backorders or special orders?
45. How do you decide when to restock? Reorder levels, manual checks, supplier visits?
46. How do you identify dead stock or slow-moving items?
47. How often do you do a stock take? Who does it, and how long does it take?
48. Do you do cycle counts or full counts?
49. How do you record stock adjustments for theft, damage, expiry, or errors? Who approves them?
50. How do you move stock between branches? Request, approval, dispatch, receive, in-transit?
51. Do you need to know stock that is in transit between branches?
52. Can one branch sell stock that belongs to another branch?
53. How do you handle multiple suppliers for the same product?
54. Do you need to track landed costs such as transport, duty, and handling?

## F. Purchasing & suppliers

55. How do you decide what to buy and how much?
56. Do you use purchase orders or LPOs? Who approves them?
57. What supplier details do you keep? Terms, credit limits, statements, payment history?
58. Do you track supplier price history?
59. How do you reconcile supplier invoices against GRNs and purchase orders?
60. How do you pay suppliers? Cash, bank, M-Pesa, cheque, credit?
61. How do you handle supplier credit notes and returns?

## G. Sales, POS & customers

62. What sales channels do you use? Walk-in, phone, WhatsApp, delivery, contracts?
63. Which documents do you issue? Quotation, proforma, invoice, receipt, delivery note?
64. Do you issue eTIMS invoices for every sale, including credit sales?
65. What payment methods do you accept? Cash, M-Pesa Till/Paybill/STK, card, bank, credit.
66. Do you allow split payments or deposits?
67. How do you track credit customers such as contractors and companies?
68. Do you need customer statements, aging reports, and credit limits?
69. How do you handle sales returns and credit notes?
70. Do you print receipts, A4 invoices, or send by SMS/WhatsApp/email?
71. Do you use cashier shifts, till opening/closing, or Z-reports?
72. Do you offer loyalty discounts or sales agent commissions?
73. Do you manage deliveries, drivers, and proof of delivery?

## H. Tax & KRA compliance

74. What is your VAT registration status?
75. Which items are standard-rated, exempt, or zero-rated? How do you classify them today?
76. How do you currently issue eTIMS invoices? Who is the buyer for cash sales, credit sales, and company sales?
77. What daily, weekly, or monthly reports do you produce for KRA or internal audit?
78. How do you handle credit notes and debit notes for KRA?
79. Do you need automatic tax reconciliation and filing support?
80. What audit trail do you need for tax purposes?
81. Are there other compliance needs? County licences, weights and measures, etc.

## I. Reporting & analytics

82. What daily reports do you need? Sales, stock, cash, credit, eTIMS?
83. Do you need branch comparison reports?
84. Do you need profit reports by product, branch, customer, or salesperson?
85. Do you need stock valuation, dead stock, and reorder reports?
86. Do you need supplier performance and customer aging reports?
87. What report do you wish you had but don’t?
88. Who needs dashboards? Should they work on phone?
89. Do you need export to Excel, PDF, email, or WhatsApp?

## J. Hardware, network & environment

90. What hardware do you already have? Computers, tablets, phones, receipt printers, barcode scanners, label printers, cash drawers, customer displays.
91. What operating systems do you use? Windows, Android, Linux?
92. How reliable is your internet and power? Do you have UPS or generator?
93. Do you need offline mode? How long should it keep working without internet?
94. How should offline data sync when the network returns?
95. Do you use a VPN or cloud service between branches?
96. What security do you need? Passwords, biometrics, CCTV, user logs?
97. How do you back up data today? How often, where, and who checks it?

## K. Migration, rollout & support

98. What data do you need migrated from the current system? Products, prices, stock, customers, suppliers, history?
99. Can the current system export data? In what format?
100. What data cleanup is needed? Duplicates, missing prices, wrong stock?
101. Should rollout be pilot at one branch first?
102. What training would staff need? Who will be the system admin?
103. What support hours do you need?
104. What is your budget and timeline?
105. What are the must-have features vs. nice-to-have features?
106. What would make this project a success for you?
107. What risks or concerns do you have?

## L. Anything else

108. Is there anything we didn’t ask that you think we should know?
109. Can we observe operations at a branch during a busy period?
110. Can we collect sample documents?  
- Product list with SKUs, prices, units  
- Branch price lists  
- Stock count sheets  
- Sample invoices, receipts, eTIMS invoices, credit notes  
- LPOs, GRNs, supplier statements  
- Customer credit list and aging  
- KRA VAT returns and eTIMS reports  
- Current system screenshots or exports  
- User roles list  
- Hardware inventory
